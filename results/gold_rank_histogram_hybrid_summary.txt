CONCLUSION
----------
HybridVerifier does NOT raise the rank-1 ceiling (avg -27.06
points) despite producing higher downstream search accuracy in SS7/SS11.
This would mean its benefit comes entirely from elsewhere (e.g. a cleaner
score gap reducing noise-driven misranking at the search level) rather
than from genuinely ranking gold relations higher.
